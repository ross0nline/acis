## Summary

Health plan compliance administrators must prepare for significant formulary, cost-sharing, and benefit design changes stemming from CMS codification of the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program under the Inflation Reduction Act, which will affect Part D plan sponsors' pricing, coverage, and reporting obligations.

Convene a cross-functional team including pharmacy benefit, actuarial, and legal staff to review proposed rule provisions, assess impacts on formulary design and negotiated drug pricing contracts, and submit formal comments to CMS during the public comment period.
