**Date Detected:** 2026-11-03
**Source:** HHS
**Risk Score:** 9/10

## Summary

Health plan administrators receiving HHS funding must revise nondiscrimination policies and procedures to comply with updated Section 504 requirements, including the Department's clarified interpretation of gender identity disorders and disability definitions, which may require updates to plan documents, member communications, and provider network standards.

## Required Action

Conduct a comprehensive review of all plan nondiscrimination policies, member-facing documents, and provider agreements to ensure alignment with the 2024 Final Rule's revised Section 504 requirements, and engage legal counsel to assess any necessary updates to disability-related coverage determinations and accommodation procedures.
