## Summary

The GLOBE Model is a mandatory Medicare Part B drug pricing payment model that will require health plan compliance administrators overseeing Medicare-aligned or supplemental products to monitor changes in drug inflation rebate calculations and assess downstream impacts on cost-sharing, benefit design, and reporting obligations.

## Required Action

Conduct an immediate impact assessment to identify all Medicare Part B drugs and biological products in your plan's formulary or supplemental coverage that fall under the GLOBE Model's scope, and engage your pharmacy and finance teams to model the financial and operational changes required by the alternative rebate calculation methodology.
