## Summary

This proposed rule modifies the limits and requirements for State directed payments in Medicaid managed care, requiring health plan compliance administrators to assess and potentially restructure payment rate methodologies and managed care contract terms to align with updated CMS interpretations of Sections 1902(a)(4) and 1903(m)(2)(A)(iii) of the Social Security Act.

## Required Action

Review all existing Medicaid managed care contracts and State directed payment arrangements against the proposed modifications to payment rate limits and submit formal comments to CMS during the public comment period to document organizational impact and seek clarification on implementation requirements.
